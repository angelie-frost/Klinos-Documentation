**Mode:** Studio, Photoreal and Playground

You can move the cards in the right panel into any order. Each mode keeps its own order.

## Move a card by dragging

1. Find the **grip** in the card's top-right corner, beside its title.
2. Drag it up or down. With a mouse, the drag starts after a small movement. On touch or pen, press and hold first.
3. Release to drop the card. Press Esc during the drag to cancel.

The card moves live as you pass its neighbours, and the panel scrolls when you reach its edge.

## Move a card with the menu or keyboard

- **Click the grip** (or press Enter on it) to open its menu: **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to top**, **Move to bottom** and **Reset order**.
- **Alt+Up** or **Alt+Down** on a focused grip moves the card one place.

Screen readers announce each move, for example "Lighting moved to position 4 of 8".

## Reset to the default order

Click **Reset card order** above the panel footer. It only appears when the current mode's order is not the default. **Reset order** in the grip menu does the same.

## Default order

| Mode | Cards, top to bottom |
|---|---|
| Studio | Device, Match a photo, Angle, Lighting, Staging, Screen, Frame, Backdrop and shadow |
| Photoreal | Device, Screen, Frame, Backdrop and shadow |
| Playground | Selection, Angle, Layouts, Background and shadow |

## Where the order is saved

The order is saved per user and per mode on your computer, like your theme and panel layout. It is not saved in the Figma file or on a mockup. If an update adds a new card, it appears at its default position in your saved order.

> **Tip**
> Put the cards you use most at the top. For lighting work, try Lighting right under Device.

## What you'll see

- While you drag, the card is outlined and lifted.
- Moves count only the cards you can see. On the MacBook, Match a photo is hidden, so it is skipped.
- Reset card order appears above the footer once you change the order.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting the order to follow the file.** It is per user, on your computer, not in the file.
- **Expecting one order for all modes.** Each mode has its own.
- **Dragging the card title.** Drag the grip in the top-right corner.
