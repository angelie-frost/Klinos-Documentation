**Mode:** Studio and Photoreal

The Screen card controls how your frame sits on the device's display.

## How your design fits

Klinos fills the display with your design. **Fill** crops the design to the display, so nothing is stretched. If your frame has a different shape from the display, the edges are cut off.

**Frame size to design at:**

- **Phones** (iPhone 13, iPhone 17 Pro, Galaxy S26): 360 wide, about 800 tall. Any frame that is 360 wide works.
- **iPad Pro 11″ and MacBook Pro 16″:** TBD.

## Scale and move the design

1. Set **Image scale** (100 to 400%) to zoom the design inside the display.
2. Once the design is larger than the screen, drag it on the glass in the preview to move it.
3. To scale with the mouse, hold Alt and scroll over the design.
4. Click **Reset** to put the scale and position back.

## Screen glare (Studio only)

**Screen glare** shows the reflection on the device's glass. It is on by default. Turn it off for a flat, glare-free screen.

Photoreal has no screen glass or reflection, so this switch only appears in Studio.

## Moving around the preview

These work in both Studio and Photoreal:

- **Scroll** zooms the preview.
- **Space + drag** or **middle-drag** pans it.

The preview zoom doesn't change the output. It only helps you inspect details.

## What you'll see

- At 100% Image scale, the design fills the display edge to edge.
- Above 100%, part of the design is outside the display, and you can drag it into view.
- In Photoreal, the help text under the slider notes that the device angle is the photograph's.

## Common mistakes

- **Designing at the wrong shape.** Fill crops anything that doesn't match the display. Check the frame shape before you design.
- **Trying to drag the design at 100%.** It only moves once it is larger than the screen. Raise Image scale first.
- **Looking for Screen glare in Photoreal.** It isn't there. The Photoreal renders have no glass reflection.
