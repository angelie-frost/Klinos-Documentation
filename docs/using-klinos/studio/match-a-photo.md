**Mode:** Studio

Match a photo reads the camera angle from a photo of a real device. You trace its screen's four corners. Klinos can then put your design into the photo, or copy the angle onto the 3D device.

It works on every Studio device except the MacBook Pro 16″. On the MacBook the card is hidden, because a traced lid mixes the body's rotation with the hinge.

## Trace a photo

1. In the Match a photo card, load a photo of a device.
2. Four colour-coded handles appear on the photo. Drag them onto the screen's corners, starting top-left.
3. Scroll to zoom in for precision.
4. Release a handle to see the fit: tilt, turn, roll and how well it fits, as a percentage.

## Use the result

- **Put your design into the photo.** Switch to the Photo view. The view showing is what gets inserted.
- **Copy the angle onto the device.** Click **Apply angle to device**. Tilt, Turn, Roll and Perspective are set from the photo, and the angle becomes Custom. Click **Undo angle** to take it back.

In the Photo view, the **Corner** slider rounds the corners of your design to match the screen. Screen glare also applies to the design.

## Other controls

- **Reset corners** puts the four handles back where they started.
- **×** removes the photo.
- If two poses fit the corners equally well, Klinos offers the mirror pose as "Try tilt / turn". Click it to swap, and click again to swap back.

> **Warning**
> Keep the corners inside the photo. A corner past the photo's edge gets an amber halo and a named warning.

> **Tip**
> Applied angles are clamped to the dial ranges. If that happens, the readout says so.

## What you'll see

- In the Photo view, the angle and shadow controls, Lighting and the backdrop rows are hidden. Trim to device stays.
- After Apply angle to device, the Angle card shows Custom.
- The readout updates each time you release a handle.

## Common mistakes

- **Using it on the MacBook.** The card isn't there for the MacBook.
- **Starting from the wrong corner.** Start at the top-left corner of the screen.
- **Inserting the wrong view.** The view showing, Device or Photo, is what gets inserted.
