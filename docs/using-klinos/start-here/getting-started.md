This page shows where to find Klinos and walks you through your first mockup.

## Open Klinos

Klinos is published in the company's Figma organization. Open it directly from Figma's plugins. You don't need to import anything.

## Your first mockup in 2 minutes

1. **Select a frame.** In Figma, click the frame you want to show on a device.
2. **Open Klinos** from your organization's plugins in Figma. The panel opens on the Studio tab. The status bar shows your frame's name and size.
3. **Pick a device.** In the Device card, choose a device and a finish. The default is the iPhone 17 Pro in Cosmic Orange.
4. **Pick an angle.** In the Angle card, choose a preset. Upright has Flat, Turn left and Turn right. Laid back has Laid left, Laid right and Steep. The default is Turn left.
5. **Choose a scale.** At the bottom of the panel, pick 1x, 2x or 4x. The default is 2x.
6. **Click Insert mockup.** Klinos places the mockup on the canvas and selects it.

## What you'll see

- The mockup is placed 80 px to the right of your frame, lined up with its top.
- It is named after your frame, for example "Home — mockup".
- If you select the mockup again later, Klinos restores its settings and the button reads **Refresh mockup**. Click it to update the mockup after you change the frame.
- With nothing selected, the status bar says "Select a frame to build a mockup from."

> **Tip**
> Need the image somewhere else? Select the mockup in Figma and export it as a PNG. Or click **Copy** next to Insert mockup and paste it. If a paste fails, export as PNG instead. See [Exporting and copy](../panel/exporting.md).

## Common mistakes

- **Selecting something Klinos can't export.** Klinos says "That selection cannot be exported. Pick a frame or group." Select a frame or group instead.
- **Deleting the source frame.** A mockup remembers the frame it came from. If that frame is gone, Klinos says "This mockup points at a frame that no longer exists. Select a frame to start again."
- **Trying to refresh a V2 mockup.** V3 can't read mockups made by V2. Make the mockup again in V3.
- **Opening the wrong plugin.** V2 and V3 are both called "Klinos". Check the build label at the right of the preview's status bar.
