Klinos is a Figma plugin built around a library of device mockups that tilt into a 3D perspective angle. It puts a Figma frame onto a device, or turns any Figma layer into a card at an angle, and places the result back on the canvas.

> **Try Klinos in your browser**
> It's the real Klinos panel, running right here in the docs, with nothing to install.
>
> [Open Try it live](../reference/try-it-live.md)

The current release is [**v3.16**](../reference/changelog.md#v316). To see which build you have, look at the small label at the right of the preview's status bar.

## Three modes

Klinos has three modes. You switch between them with the tabs at the top of the panel.

:::cards
- **Studio**: 3D device mockups with editable lighting.
- **Photoreal**: Static renders of real devices with your design keyed into the screen.
- **Playground**: Any Figma layer as an angled card, or several layers as a layout.
:::

## Where your settings live

- **On the mockup.** Each mockup keeps its settings. Select it later and Klinos restores them, so you can refresh it in place.
- **On your computer.** Your theme, panel layout and card order are kept per user, not in the Figma file.

> **Warning**
> Mockups made with the older Klinos V2 can't be refreshed by this version. Make them again in V3.
