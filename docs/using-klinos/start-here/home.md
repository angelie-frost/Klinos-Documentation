Klinos is our in-house Figma plugin for making mockups. It puts a Figma frame onto a device, or turns any Figma layer into a card at an angle, and places the result back on the canvas.

The packaged build is **v3.15**. To see which build you have, look at the small label at the right of the preview's status bar.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): open the plugin and make your first mockup.
- [Device library](../reference/device-library.md): every device, its finishes and its sizes.
- [Changelog](../reference/changelog.md): what changed in each build.

## Three modes

Klinos has three modes. You switch between them with the tabs at the top of the panel.

### Studio

3D device mockups with editable lighting. The devices are the iPhone 13, iPhone 17 Pro, Galaxy S26, MacBook Pro 16″ (lid 0–135°) and iPad Pro 11″ (portrait or landscape).

### Photoreal

Static front-on renders of real devices: the iPhone 17 Pro in two finishes and the MacBook Pro 14″. Your design is keyed into the screen. There is no angle, and no screen glass or reflection.

### Playground

Unreleased. Merged to main, ships in the next package (v3.16).

Any Figma element as a flat card at an angle: an image, shape, vector, text, frame, component or instance. Select two or more layers to arrange them as a layout. There is no device, no material and no light.

## Where your settings live

- **On the mockup.** Each mockup keeps its settings. Select it later and Klinos restores them, so you can refresh it in place.
- **On your computer.** Your theme, panel layout and card order are kept per user, not in the Figma file.

> **Warning**
> Mockups made with the older Klinos V2 can't be refreshed by this version. Make them again in V3.
