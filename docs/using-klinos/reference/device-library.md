Every device Klinos can render, in Studio and Photoreal. Sizes are in millimetres, from the plugin's own geometry.

## Studio devices

### iPhone 17 Pro

- **Picker line:** 6.3″ display · 2025
- **Finishes:** Silver, Cosmic Orange (default), Deep Blue
- **Body:** 71.9 × 150.0 × 8.75 mm
- **Display:** 66.592 × 144.776 mm
- **Design frame:** 360 wide, about 800 tall. Any frame that is 360 wide works.
- **Options:** Polished frame, Surface texture (default 65%, whole unibody), Match a photo
- **Brightness:** up to 105%
- **Notes:** The camera plateau is part of the model.

### iPhone 13

- **Picker line:** 6.1″ display · 2021
- **Finishes:** Midnight (default), Blue, Pink, Starlight
- **Body:** 71.5 × 146.7 × 7.65 mm
- **Display:** 66.7 × 141.9 mm
- **Design frame:** 360 wide, about 800 tall. Any frame that is 360 wide works.
- **Options:** Polished frame, Surface texture (default 45%, aluminium frame and buttons only), Match a photo
- **Brightness:** up to 110%
- **Notes:** Midnight and Pink are colour-matched to photos. Blue and Starlight use Apple's swatch colours with the same conversion, because no photo was available.

### Galaxy S26

- **Picker line:** 6.3″ display · 2026
- **Finishes:** Black (default), Silver Shadow, Sky Blue
- **Body:** 71.7 × 149.6 × 7.2 mm
- **Display:** 66.74 × 144.61 mm
- **Design frame:** 360 wide, about 800 tall. Any frame that is 360 wide works.
- **Options:** Polished frame, Surface texture (default 55%, frame and frosted back), Match a photo
- **Brightness:** up to 105%
- **Notes:** Black is fitted to Samsung's product views. Silver Shadow and Sky Blue use Samsung's swatch colours with the same conversion, because no photo was available. New in V3, with no V2 version.

### MacBook Pro 16″

- **Picker line:** 16.2″ display · adjustable lid
- **Finishes:** Space Black (default), Silver
- **Base:** 355.7 × 248.1 × 11.95 mm
- **Lid:** 248.1 long, 3.3 thick (mm)
- **Display:** 345.6 × 223.4 mm
- **Design frame:** TBD
- **Options:** Lid angle 0° to 135° (default 110°), Surface texture (default 100%, whole body)
- **Not available:** Polished frame, Match a photo
- **Brightness:** up to 110%
- **Notes:** Exports at three times the phone base size. The right-side ports and their insides are estimates.

### iPad Pro 11″

- **Picker line:** 11″ display · portrait or landscape
- **Finishes:** Space Black (default), Silver
- **Body:** 177.5 × 249.7 × 5.3 mm
- **Display:** 160.5 × 232.8 mm
- **Design frame:** TBD
- **Options:** Landscape, Surface texture (default 85%, whole body), Match a photo
- **Not available:** Polished frame
- **Brightness:** up to 110%
- **Notes:** Exports at twice the phone base size, so its display isn't a downsample of your design.

## Photoreal renders

These are photographic renders, not the Studio models. Each is one front-on view.

### iPhone 17 Pro (Photoreal)

- **Finishes:** Cosmic Orange, Deep Blue
- **Design frame:** 360 wide, about 800 tall. Any frame that is 360 wide works.
- **Notes:** No screen glass or reflection.

### MacBook Pro 14″ (Photoreal)

- **Finishes:** Silver
- **Design frame:** TBD
- **Notes:** No screen glass or reflection. Photoreal has the 14″, Studio has the 16″.

The renders are stored larger than Figma's 4096 px limit (2600 × 5284 px for the phone, 4174 × 2522 px for the MacBook), so exports are never upscaled.
