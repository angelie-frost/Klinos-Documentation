## Where to report

Send bug reports and feedback directly to the plugin's maintainer. There is no in-app feedback yet. It is planned for the future.

## What to include

1. **The build.** The small label at the right of the preview's status bar, for example v3.15.
2. **The mode.** Studio, Photoreal or Playground.
3. **What you rendered.**
   - Studio: the device, finish, Look and angle preset.
   - Photoreal: the device and finish.
   - Playground: the layout, and the kinds of layers you selected.
4. **The scale.** 1x, 2x or 4x.
5. **What you did.** The steps, in order.
6. **What you expected, and what happened.**
7. **A screenshot.** Show the panel and the preview, or the inserted result.
8. **The exact status bar message,** if there was one, such as "Render failed: …".

## Extra details for some problems

- **Hidden layers in Playground:** copy the text in the hidden layer's row in the Selection card. It shows what Figma exported.
- **Exploded layers:** say whether the frame uses masks, auto layout or instances.
- **Copy and paste:** say where you pasted, for example the Figma canvas or a document.

> **Tip**
> One problem per report makes it easier to fix.
