## Colour and lighting

- **For accurate colour, use Calibrated at 100% brightness.** The finishes were fitted under that light. Every other Look is art direction.
- **Pick the Look before you touch the backdrop.** A Look replaces the backdrop colour, sweep and reflection.
- **Judge highlights on Grey or Dark.** Set Preview background to Grey or Dark, then back to Output to check what you will get.
- **For Polished frame, use Stage, Keynote or Studio White.** Their sharp lights give crisp lines on the frame. Calibrated is softer.

## Framing

- **Design at the display's shape.** Fill crops anything that doesn't match the display.
- **Show the rails for Polished frame.** Turn, Laid and Steep angles show the frame's edge. A straight-on front view hides it.
- **Keep Tilt and Turn under 90°.** Past 90° you see the back of the device and your design faces away.

## Quality

- **Export at 2x or more to see Surface texture.** At small sizes the grain is finer than a pixel.
- **Don't pick 4x for the iPad or MacBook expecting more pixels than 2x.** Both reach Figma's 4096 px limit at 2x.
- **Wait for the preview to settle before judging edges.** While a control moves, the preview uses fewer samples and edges look stepped for a moment.

## Working with mockups

- **Refresh instead of inserting again.** Select the mockup, change it, and click Refresh mockup (or Refresh image in Playground). You keep one layer.
- **Keep the source frame.** A mockup remembers the frame it came from. If you delete the frame, you can't refresh the mockup.
- **Export as PNG when Copy won't paste.** Insert the mockup, select it in Figma and export it as a PNG. Copy is quicker but may not paste everywhere.

## Playground

- **Set Perspective, Tilt and Turn to 0 for editable layers.** Only then can Arc, Fan and Stack go in as layers. Use Roll for angle.
- **Use Depth dim, not Depth fade, in Stack.** Fade lets the cards behind show through.
- **Give Glass a darker background.** Glass is faint on light and transparent backgrounds.
- **Keep the card count low with Glass.** The blur gets slower with more cards.
- **Avoid blend modes on Playground layers.** They are lost when a layer is exported on its own.
- **Name layers before curating Exploded's layer list.** Renaming a layer drops its saved choice.
